About

In my own words

I grew up in Manhattan, where you learn independence early. As a kid I asked for drones and engineering kits instead of dolls, and I would spend hours working out how things were put together. That habit never went away. It moved to code.

I started college in Computer Science and moved to Information Science, which lets me combine my interest in technology with my love of building. With Claude Code I automated daily work across 250+ devices at GlareSquare, and I built a fashion app to solve a problem I had myself.

My mom is from Denmark, so Danish culture has always been part of my life. In summer 2025 I lived on my own in Copenhagen, took classes at Copenhagen Business School and worked at UNIQLO. I also studied abroad in Madrid for a semester. When I need to clear my head, I go for a run or a long walk.
